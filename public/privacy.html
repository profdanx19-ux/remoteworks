Privacy Policy

RemoteStaff Hub values the privacy and trust of applicants, clients, and visitors. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you apply for a role through this website.

Information We Collect

We may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, country, city, hours available, work availability, internet status, experience details, and any assessment answers you submit as part of the application process.

How We Use Information

  • To review and evaluate job applications.
  • To contact applicants about role suitability, interview steps, and assessment links.
  • To maintain records of recruitment and hiring activity.
  • To improve the quality and fairness of our hiring process.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with internal recruiting staff, authorized hiring partners, or trusted service providers only where necessary to process applications or support the recruitment workflow.

Cookies and Analytics

This site may use cookies or analytics tools to understand traffic, improve site performance, and monitor general user behavior. These tools help us improve the hiring experience and maintain the site’s functionality.

Data Retention

We retain application and assessment information only as long as needed for recruitment, compliance, and legitimate business purposes. We may delete or anonymize data when it is no longer needed.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to help protect personal data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

Your Rights
